An Bhean-Fhlâneur

— the woman flâneur —

Seven women walk Dublin — a city built by men, for men. This is a film about what catches their attention when they look up.

Take part Support the film Read the journal

The idea

Start with a map of Dublin. Draw a circle on it. The circle becomes a walking route, and the route is walked by a woman who lives in this city — reading the streets in her own way.

Circle by circle, the routes overlap like ripples spreading across the city. What links them is what the women notice: above the shopfronts, where the ghost signs and old stonework survive, there's another Dublin — centuries deep — that everyone walks past.

The film shows the city through their eyes, and their stories surface through what catches their attention along the way.

Take part

We're looking for seven women who walk, roll or wander Dublin. However long you've been here — a lifetime, a year, or a week — and however you got here, the film starts with what you notice.

You might be seeing the city for the first time. You might know its strangest corners, or have a route no one else could invent. You might move through it differently than it was built for. There's no one way to be a Dubliner, and no one way to walk one.

No experience needed — we're not looking for performers.

To apply, fill in a short questionnaire — about twenty minutes — and record a voice note telling us about a walk you know. That's all.

Open the questionnaire

Your answers go only to the production team, are stored in the EU, and are used solely for casting this project. You can ask us to delete them at any time.
